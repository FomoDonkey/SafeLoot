Your loot finally has a guardian.

You joined a group of strangers. Two hours in, the item you came for drops, and at that very moment the leader switches to master loot. Or the mage rolls Need on plate. Or someone "reserves" it after it drops and offers to sell it to you.

SafeLoot is the chest that watches. It tells you the second the rules change, flags the rolls that make no sense, keeps the agreement everyone accepted, and remembers who you met, so the next time you see them you already know.

It works even if you are the only one in the group who has it. No account, no website, no extra program. And it never writes in chat unless you press a button. The code is open source.

What it does for you

The Watcher: the alarm nobody can turn off behind your back

  • A big warning in the middle of the screen, a sound, and a panel with buttons the moment the leader changes the loot rules: master loot, free for all, a new master looter or a higher quality threshold.
  • One click to ask the group, save the evidence or accept the change if it was agreed.
  • A small bar shows the rules in force while you are grouped. It turns red if they are not the agreed ones.
  • Joining a group that already uses master loot? You are told on the spot.

The Detector: rolls that make no sense

  • Flags Need rolls on items the roller's class can never use, and rolls that break the agreed rules.
  • It shows you, it never accuses anyone by itself. If it gets one wrong, It's a mistake teaches it.

The Rules: agree before the run, in one line

  • Choose the dungeon, choose who needs what (MS>OS, all Greed, BoE on Greed) and publish one chat line everyone can read, with real item links.
  • Every ok is recorded with its time. 2/3 agreed, at a glance.
  • If the leader writes HR, SR or MS>OS in chat, SafeLoot offers to use it as the rules.

Loot by boss: pick, don't type

  • The 28 dungeons of WoW: Forever, the nine new ones included, and Onyxia's Lair, sorted by level, with their bosses.
  • Open a boss, hover an item, click to reserve it for yourself or for anyone in your group. HR or SR.
  • Items really seen dropping by you or by SafeLoot users in your group are confirmed; the rest are marked not seen yet.

The address book: know who you are playing with

  • Right-click any player and mark them: Trusted, Ninja, Sells loot or Quits.
  • From then on you see it everywhere, with an icon and a word: tooltip, a badge under your target, the Group Finder, invitations, whispers, and the moment they join your group.
  • Categories with counters, a card for each player, notes, search, and your current group always on top. Hundreds of names without the mess.

The Journal: what happened, in writing

  • After each run: whether the rules were kept, who got what, and a plain-text copy ready for Discord.

New here? Don't trust us, check it

SafeLoot is a new add-on, and it is normal to be careful with something you have never heard of. Everything below can be checked by you, or by anyone you know who reads code.

  • Open source. The code is public and MIT licensed: github.com/FomoDonkey/SafeLoot. No hidden or scrambled code: every file in the download is plain text you can open, except three small images.
  • Nothing to install outside the game. No .exe, no launcher, no website account, no login. The download is 35 Lua files, 1 XML, 3 images, 2 TOC files and the licences.
  • It cannot see your password, your files or the internet. The game runs every add-on in a closed box, and SafeLoot is no exception.
  • It cannot act on anyone. There is no code in it to kick, invite, ignore, report, trade, mail, roll or pass. Search for it.
  • Only three things can be written in chat, and each one needs your click: publishing the rules, asking the group about a change, and telling the group about a roll.
  • What leaves your PC: item numbers per boss, shared with other SafeLoot users in your group (you can turn it off), and a small test ping if you press the self-test. Never names, marks or notes.
  • Tested. The test suite is public, and 0.5.1 passed its 657 automatic checks before it was uploaded. What we have not yet seen working in the real game is listed below, in plain words.
  • Easy to leave. Delete the folders SafeLoot and SafeLoot_LootData and it is gone. Only its own settings file stays in your WTF folder, until you delete it.

Something you don't like, or a doubt? Ask in the comments or on GitHub.

Start in one minute

  1. Install it and log in.
  2. Click the button on the minimap, or type /safeloot (or /sl).
  3. Join a group. That is all: the watcher, the detector and the journal work by themselves.

Want to see the alarm before you need it? Type /safeloot alarm.

Fair by design

  • Private. Your marks and notes stay on your PC. There are no public lists of players and there never will be.
  • Never acts for you. It does not kick, block, report or roll. Nothing goes to chat without your click.
  • SafeLoot users in the same group share item numbers per boss, never names, notes or chat. You can turn it off.
  • Light. No libraries, and nothing runs every frame in the background.

It feels like part of the game

The game's own windows, fonts, icons and sounds. Movable windows, adjustable size, large text, reduced motion, a spoken alarm, and never colour alone to tell you something.

In 5 languages: English, Español, Deutsch, Français and Português (Brasil).

Beta: what we know and what we are still confirming

SafeLoot is young and we prefer to tell you the truth. Confirmed in the real game: the alarm, the rules bar, the rules agreed by party chat, the roll history in the open world, the journal and the minimap button. Still being confirmed: boss loot and the alarm inside dungeons and raids, and the new address book. Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit will be added to the lists when their data is available.

Found something wrong? Leave a comment or open an issue on GitHub. /safeloot test gives you a report to copy.

Questions

Does my group need it? No. The chat line is plain text with item links: anyone can read it and answer ok.

Can it stop a ninja? No add-on can. It makes sure you find out in time, you have it in writing, and you remember.

I am the leader. Is it for me? Yes: publish the rules in one click and show your group you play fair.

How do I get help? /safeloot help explains everything in the game, with a glossary (HR, SR, MS>OS, BoE...).

En español

Por fin alguien vigila tu botín.

Te has unido a un grupo de desconocidos. Dos horas después cae el objeto por el que viniste y, justo entonces, el líder cambia a maestro despojador. O el mago tira Necesidad en placas. O alguien lo «reserva» cuando ya ha caído y te lo quiere vender.

SafeLoot es el cofre que vigila. Te avisa en el segundo en que cambian las reglas, marca las tiradas que no tienen sentido, guarda lo que el grupo acordó y recuerda a quién conociste. Funciona aunque solo lo tengas tú y nunca escribe en el chat sin que pulses un botón.

  • Alarma si cambian las reglas del botín, con aviso en pantalla, sonido y botones para preguntar, guardar la prueba o aceptar el cambio.
  • Detector de Necesidades imposibles para la clase.
  • Reglas y reservas en una línea de chat con enlaces de objeto, y cada «ok» con su hora.
  • Botín por jefe de las 28 mazmorras de Forever y Onyxia: eliges el objeto en vez de escribirlo.
  • Agenda de jugadores: clic derecho para marcar Ninja, Confianza, Vende botín o Abandona, y lo verás en todas partes.
  • Diario de cada grupo, listo para copiar en Discord.

¿Desconfías? Es normal, compruébalo: código abierto y legible en GitHub, sin programas, cuentas ni librerías. No tiene código para expulsar, denunciar, comerciar ni tirar dados, y solo escribe en el chat cuando pulsas un botón. Para quitarlo, borra las carpetas SafeLoot y SafeLoot_LootData.

Abre el addon con el botón del minimapa o con /safeloot. Tus marcas y notas no salen de tu PC y no hay listas públicas de jugadores. Es una beta: el botín de jefes y la alarma dentro de mazmorras y bandas todavía se están confirmando en el juego real.
